An offline guide and tracker for every one of Grand Theft Auto VI's 55 PlayStation 5
trophies: 1 platinum, 2 gold, 6 silver and 46 bronze, 28 of them hidden on PlayStation
until earned. Open `app/index.html` in a desktop browser — no build step, no network,
no account. It works with the network off.

## What it does

- **Checklist with saved progress.** Tick a trophy off and it stays ticked
  (localStorage). Export your progress to a file and import it back on any machine.
- **Every trophy with its official name, description and grade**, as PlayStation
  publishes them, cross-checked on Exophase and PSNProfiles.
- **A guide in plain words for each trophy**, naming its sources beside it —
  PowerPyx, Push Square, KeenGamer, GameMapa, Insider Gaming and others. Five hidden
  trophies have no verified method in any published guide yet, and this guide says
  so instead of inventing one.
- **Roadmap to the platinum** in four stages, built from the official descriptions:
  story first (60–80 hours), side strands, the 100% completion push, then the
  odd one-offs. PowerPyx puts the whole run at roughly 200 hours, Push Square at
  300 or more. All 55 trophies are offline — there are no online trophies.
- **Missable flags.** One trophy is flagged as potentially missable: Positive
  Mental Attitude, whose Chapter 1 balcony meditation is only available before
  leaving Chapter 1. Character-locked strands are called out too: Andy is
  Lucia-only, Tyrone and Bret are Jason-only.
- **Filters** by kind (story, missable, collectibles, activities, online), by grade,
  and by search; **a spoiler setting** that hides the names and descriptions of
  hidden trophies until they are earned.

## Try it

Serve `app/` with any static server, or run `node serve.ts` and open
http://localhost:8080. Add `?sample=1` to the URL to see the guide with twelve
trophies already ticked; `?open=28` opens the guide for Positive Mental Attitude;
`?view=roadmap` jumps to the roadmap; `?filter=story` filters to story trophies.
