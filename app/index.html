Roadmap to the platinum — Vice Got a Price!

PowerPyx puts the whole run at roughly 200 hours, Push Square at 300 or more. One playthrough, all 55 trophies offline, no online trophies. The order below is built from the official descriptions.

  1. Stage 1 — Play the story, don't rush

    Chapters 1–4 and the story missions. Try activities as they unlock, scan with WAiNK as you travel, and start side strands when they appear. Do the Chapter 1 balcony meditation for Positive Mental Attitude before leaving Chapter 1 — it is the one trophy that can be missed.

  2. Stage 2 — Finish the story and the side strands

    Final mission, then the side-character finales (Andy, Kenton, Billy, Randy, Tyrone), the 8 stranger strands, and the robbery and partner trophies. Character-locked strands need the right protagonist: Andy is Lucia-only, Tyrone and Bret are Jason-only.

  3. Stage 3 — The 100% completion push

    After the story, open Progress, then Stats, then the 100% Completion checklist. Stunts, shooting-range golds, drone races, card sets, WAiNK scans, challenges, vehicles, areas, garages and the money trophies. This is most of the hours.

  4. Stage 4 — Odd jobs and cleanup

    The one-off feats: Combat Focus, the VCIA departure, the showroom, bowling, the unconfirmed hidden trophies — then the platinum pops.

Sources and honesty note

Official names, descriptions and grades are as PlayStation publishes them, cross-checked on Exophase and PSNProfiles. Guides are written for this entry in its own words; each trophy names its sources beside its guide. Five hidden trophies — In The Beginning…, Capital Offense, Bad Luck, I Guess, The Truth Is Out There and Ten Pin — have no verified method in any published guide yet (GameMapa lists them as still unsolved), and this guide says so instead of inventing one. Counts such as 397 stunts, 42 shooting-range challenges and 18 card sets come from GameMapa, Push Square and KeenGamer.
